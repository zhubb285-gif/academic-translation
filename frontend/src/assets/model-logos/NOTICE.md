# AI brand marks

SVG artwork from `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` v1.95.1 (LobeHub, MIT):
https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons
https://www.npmjs.com/package/@lobehub/icons-static-svg

The marks identify their respective AI products and owners. Local SVG assets
avoid third-party image requests. The single-colour versions inherit colours from
the card palette. Anthropic uses the Claude product mark; Google uses Gemini.
